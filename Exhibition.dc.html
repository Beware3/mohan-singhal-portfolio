Room V

About the artist

I photograph to slow myself down. I started with birds because they were the only thing in my neighbourhood that would not wait for me, and I have been trying to catch up ever since — across more than fifty places, most of them in India, usually alone and usually early.

I am not interested in the rarest animal or the most dramatic sky. I am interested in the moment a place forgets I am in it. That is the same whether I am on a forest track at first light, on a rooftop at two in the morning waiting out a long exposure, or standing in a lane while a barber finishes a shave.

Everything here is made with available light and printed small — the way a field notebook should be read.

Based in Bengaluru · Working since 2016
